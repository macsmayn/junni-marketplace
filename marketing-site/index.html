How it works
01

Upload the statements

Financial statements and tax returns, as they arrive from the borrower. No template to fill in first.

02

Figures are spread and confirmed

Junni extracts the numbers into a structured statement. You review and correct anything it read wrong before it scores.

03

Metrics are computed and graded

Ratios are calculated by formula, then graded against thresholds set for that industry.

04

The memo comes out finished

Executive summary, metric tables, strengths and risks, benchmarks and diligence questions. Export to PDF or Word.

Why it holds up

A score you can defend in front of a credit committee

Most AI credit tools hand you a number and no way to argue with it. Junni computes the score arithmetically.

The language model writes the narrative. It does not decide the score.

Thresholds are set per industry across 21 sectors.
